Privacy Policy for Huichol 3D

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Introduction

At Huichol 3D, we respect your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle information in our iOS application for creating 3D art inspired by Wixárika (Huichol) culture.

Information We Collect

Huichol 3D does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information. All your 3D art projects are saved locally on your device.

Data Storage

All information related to your artistic creations, saved projects, and settings is stored only on your iOS device. We do not have access to this information, nor is it transmitted to any external servers.

In-App Purchases

Huichol 3D offers an in-app purchase to unlock unlimited projects. All transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information or financial data. Purchases are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

Huichol 3D does NOT use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any information.

Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. Parents can allow their children to use the app with complete peace of mind.

App Permissions

Huichol 3D uses the following system features:

  • Screen recording: The app can record video of your 3D painting session using ReplayKit. These videos are stored locally on your device and are not sent to any server. You control when to start and stop recording.
  • Content sharing: You can share snapshots of your 3D projects using iOS's standard share menu. You control with whom and how you share these images.

The app does NOT request access to camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo library.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or Huichol 3D, you can contact us at spellingtrainerapp@gmail.com

Consent

By using Huichol 3D, you consent to this privacy policy.
